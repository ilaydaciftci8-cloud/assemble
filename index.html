For Founders

Four months here replace the year you'd lose finding the right people alone.

Every meeting is chosen for where your business is right now. You come with a list of who you should be talking to — we challenge it, in case the room you're avoiding is the one that moves you forward, then put you in the right one. You won't need the same meetings whether you're raising your first round, hiring your 15th employee, or closing an enterprise-scale contract. Time is your most valuable resource: you only get the opportunities made for you.

You need to sell.
Start with the rooms that took us years to open.

Meet executives from La Banque Postale, Société Générale, Allianz, Capgemini, Kiabi, Sanofi — and the wider network of HEC Paris partners and alumni. We assess what's on the other side of the table and bring you in only when there's a real reason for you both to be there.

  • A buyer who's ready — a decision-maker actively looking for what you offer, briefed on you before you walk in.
  • A challenger who knows your market — an executive from the industry you're cracking, telling you off the record what you're getting wrong, what breaks when you scale, and which door to knock on next.

You leave with a live opportunity, or the insider knowledge that shortens the path to the next one.

You need to raise.
We make sure investors meet you on your best day.

We introduce you to investors chosen for your thesis, once you're actually ready.

  • Ready first — we get you investor-ready in-house, so you walk in fundable.
  • Matched, not mass-mailed — one-to-one intros built on what each investor actually backs, plus small sessions where a single investor sees three or four founders pre-selected for their exact thesis.

Hiring early is slow, expensive, and a gamble. We filter it and shorten it.

Every year, 2,300 man-days of student workforce from design, communication and sales schools work for you as part of their curriculum — most of them near graduation, coached by experts.

Your next hire: a job board reaching 5,000+ startup-minded candidates, already filtered — HEC MBAs with ten years of experience, ready to run your finance, strategy or business development for a fraction of market rate, plus access to sought-after grande école interns.

For Corporates

Your comex can tour a startup campus anywhere. The real question is what they can use on Monday.

A tour inspires for an afternoon and fades by the weekend. What your leadership needs is a sharper instinct for spotting real innovation, and direct lines to the startups that could matter to your business. It stays productive because we prepare it against your priorities in advance — your teams work on your questions, with content chosen for you.

The value of this trip is the rooms your founders walk into — rooms they could never open on their own.

We put your founders in front of the people who decide whether a solution enters the French market. On most tours, a delegation meets people whose job is to encourage them. Your founders meet people who came because they're genuinely looking for solutions that could change their industry.

Why these rooms, and why those people showed up

The quality comes from the agency's top alumni community — the people who let your founders reach into a European market that guards its introductions closely. If a founder's solution fits what these decision-makers need, that's the start of something real. If it doesn't fit yet, these are the exact people who'll tell them how to adapt it for the French market.

We know who makes it here, and who doesn't

Through delegations like Cosmet, Jetro and the Indian cohorts, we've seen clearly what separates the ones who break through from the ones who stall — and coach your founders toward the first group before they ever sit down. The founders who win treat this as the start of a relationship, not a transaction. Real deals tend to land around six months out, once they've done the follow-through.

What a week actually looks like
01

Establish the strategy

Ecosystem discovery from a leading figure in French tech, a go-to-market strategy grounded in real market insight, and lunches connecting founders to the right organizations.

02

Confront it with the market

One-to-one matchmaking with sector experts, live cold-calling and outreach clinics, and VC masterclasses on how French and European funding works.

03

Prove it

Pitch coaching and the introductions that matter. For longer programs (two weeks and up), this culminates in a demo day in front of real investors.

We build it with you, to your budget

We co-design the week around your founders, then run it end to end. Formats scale from a single immersive day to a full month — we cut or add modules to fit what you can spend. You give us the outcome you need and the number you have; we design backward from there.

Your founders come home with a market they understand, access they can call on, and a clear read on whether Europe is theirs to win. That's what turns into deals.
